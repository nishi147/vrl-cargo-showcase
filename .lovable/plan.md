# VRL Cargo landing page

## Goal
Create a modern, responsive landing page for VRL Cargo Packers & Movers, using the reference site’s services, trust signals, phone-first conversion flow, and orange/navy identity while improving clarity and polish.

## What I’ll build
- A crisp header with brand mark, simple navigation, and prominent call/quote actions.
- A high-impact first section featuring movers, cargo imagery, trust messaging, and a short quote form.
- Scannable credibility statistics covering years, moves, branches, workforce, warehousing, and vehicles.
- A six-service section for home, office, car, warehouse, insurance, and pet relocation.
- A focused “why choose us” section highlighting safe packing, tracked transport, insured handling, and timely delivery.
- A visual moving-process section, customer reassurance, FAQ, and strong closing enquiry area.
- Mobile call and WhatsApp actions that remain easy to reach.

## Visual direction
- Confident logistics aesthetic: deep navy, vivid cargo orange, clean white, and restrained red accents.
- Strong editorial typography, crisp borders, angled details, and real-world moving imagery.
- More whitespace and clearer hierarchy than the reference, without losing its energetic local-business character.

## Interaction and quality
- Working anchor navigation, quote form behavior, FAQ accordions, click-to-call links, and WhatsApp contact.
- Responsive layouts for phone, tablet, and desktop with accessible labels and keyboard states.
- Page-specific search and social metadata.
- Verify the final page visually and check for errors.

## Assumptions
- Use the reference site’s public brand name, service offering, and primary phone number.
- The quote form will provide a clear client-side confirmation; no enquiry storage or message delivery is included unless requested later.
